Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Blocks collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no network code in the app at all.

What stays on your device

Your current game, your best scores, your last fifty finished runs and your settings are written to a single file inside the app’s own storage. Nothing reads that file except Blocks, and it is never uploaded anywhere.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app makes no requests to any server, ours or anyone else’s. Tapping a link in Settings opens this website in your browser; from that point your browser, not the app, is doing the talking.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. There is nothing left behind and nothing for us to delete on your behalf, because we never had it.

Children

Blocks is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that leaves the device — a leaderboard, say — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the release notes rather than only here.
