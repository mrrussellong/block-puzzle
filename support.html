Support

Last updated 11 October 2026

Getting help

Email mrrussellong@gmail.com. Please say which device you are on and which version of Blocks you are running — the version number is at the bottom of the Settings panel.

Common questions

I closed the app mid-game. Is my run gone? No. The board is saved whenever the app leaves the screen, and reopening it puts you back where you were.

Can I turn off the animations? Yes — Settings has a Reduce motion switch, and it works whether or not the system-wide setting is on.

I find the colours hard to tell apart. Turn on “Shape markers on blocks” in Settings: each colour also gets a distinct shape. The Paper theme has the widest separation for a light room, and Dusk the widest overall.

The haptics switch does nothing on my iPad. iPads have no haptic engine. The switch is there so the setting carries across devices.

How do I start over? Pause, then “Start a new run”. Pausing on its own never costs you the board.

Reporting a bug

If a run ended when it should not have, or a piece would not place where it clearly fit, a screenshot helps a great deal. Send it to the address above.
